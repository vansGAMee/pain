Pain — Minimalist Audio Player & Sync Companion
=================================================

How to Run on Windows (10 / 11):
Option A (Standalone — No Node.js required):
1. Double-click "Pain.exe" (or "Pain-App.vbs").
2. The companion will automatically start in app mode or open in your browser at http://localhost:3000.

Option B (Batch script):
1. Double-click "run-windows.bat".

Syncing with Android:
1. Open Pain on your Android phone (ensure both PC and phone are on the same Wi-Fi).
2. The companion will automatically detect your phone and synchronize your library and statistics.
3. Likes and playback statistics synchronize automatically 2-way between PC and Android!

Ports:
- HTTP Companion: 3000
- Mobile Sync Server: 8865
- UDP Discovery: 8866

Windows hotfix 1.7.4:
- EXE and ZIP are alternatives: run one copy from a permanent folder.
- If an older version keeps opening windows, end all Pain/Pain-v*.exe processes
  in Task Manager (or restart Windows), then replace the old EXE with this one.
- Keep the data, downloads, cache and covers folders to preserve your library.
- Unpin the old taskbar icon once. Launch the updated Pain.exe, wait a few
  seconds, and pin Pain again (or pin Pain from the Start menu).
- The taskbar icon is kept in LocalAppData/Pain/shell, outside Downloads.
